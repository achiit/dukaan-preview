About 7 Star

Fifteen years of welcoming local guests.

Since 2011, 7 Star Hotel & Restaurant has been serving guests in Naya Bass, Churu. The aim is simple: make a meal, stay or family occasion feel comfortable without making the process complicated.

Over the years, the place has grown into more than a restaurant — with hotel rooms, group dining, event enquiries and on-site parking under one roof. Whether you are stopping by for food or planning a special day, our team is ready to help with the practical details.
